================================================================
DETAILED DESCRIPTION
================================================================

Play Console gives you two ways to price a product in 150 countries: one price
everywhere, or 150 edits by hand.

Pinto gives you the third. Select your markets, choose how the price should
change, look at the result, apply. Minutes instead of an afternoon.


CHARGE LESS WHERE PEOPLE EARN LESS

One price cannot be right in both Norway and Nigeria. Pinto sorts every country
into five purchasing-power bands and builds the ladder for you — you pick how
steep it goes, from gentle to aggressive.

Then you edit it. Every band, every country, every price. It is a starting
point, not a verdict.

This is the feature Play Console does not have, and the reason most people
install Pinto.


SIX WAYS TO SET A PRICE

Raise or lower by a percentage. Multiply. Set one fixed amount. Copy one
country's price to the rest. Build your own tiers. Or write a formula.

Prices convert into each market's own currency using Google's rates, and land
on endings that look like pricing — .99, .49, or whatever you use.


PICK YOUR MARKETS IN SECONDS

Search, continent, sub-region, currency, or one country at a time. Save the
markets you price alike as a named group and reuse it forever.


SEE IT BEFORE YOU SEND IT

Every change goes through Review first: old price, new price, difference, one
row per country. Warnings are flagged. Anything invalid is blocked before it
reaches Google.

Then apply, and get a straight answer about what happened — including which
countries Google refused, and why.

Wrong call? Undo restores the prices exactly as they were.


ALSO IN THE BOX

- Presets you can save, export and share with your team
- History of every operation, with one-click restore
- CSV export for the spreadsheet someone will ask for
- Dry run, to rehearse without sending anything
- A built-in guide, in English or French


YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS

Pinto has no server. No analytics, no telemetry, no account to create. Prices
go from your browser straight to Google and nowhere else — the developer never
sees them, because there is nothing to see them with.

Your sign-in token lives in memory and disappears when Chrome closes.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Pinto needs three things: a Play Console account that can edit prices, the Play
Developer API switched on, and your own Google API key pasted in once.

About five minutes, and the guide walks you through it. Worth knowing up front
— because Pinto keeps no server, it cannot hold that key for you. The upside is
that the access is yours, and you can revoke it whenever you like.


GOOD TO KNOW

Pinto sets base prices for subscriptions and one-time products. It does not
touch promotional offers or prepaid plans, and it does not change what existing
subscribers already pay — Google handles that separately, with its own notice
rules.
